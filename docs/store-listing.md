## Description (max 10,000): 1723

```text
Battery Care Portal helps you look after your laptop battery. It sets a charge limit so the battery rests at a healthy level while you stay plugged in, and it shows what your laptop is doing so you can see the effect.

What it does
- Holds charging near a limit you choose, so the battery is not kept at 100% all day.
- Tops up to full when you need it, for example before a trip, then puts your limit back.
- Shows battery health, temperature and power flow in plain words.
- Keeps a history of charge and health over time, on your PC.
- Heat protection can lower the charge limit while the laptop is hot.
- A tray pop-up shows your battery at a glance. Keyboard shortcuts and screen reader labels are included.

What is supported
- Verified: Lenovo IdeaPad and Yoga laptops with conservation mode.
- Beta: Dell, HP, ASUS and some other makes. These stay off until you opt in. The ASUS support can only read the setting, so it cannot confirm that a change worked.
- Any other laptop opens in monitor mode, which still shows health, power flow and history.

Built to be careful
- Every change is read back to check it worked.
- On a laptop that has not been verified, the app will not change anything unless you turn on beta testing.
- Your charge limit is put back when you close the app and after a crash.
- Battery Care Portal looks after charging. Power Plan Manager, a separate app, looks after power plans and fans. The two apps detect each other so they do not clash.

Privacy
There is no account and there are no ads. Your battery history and settings stay on your PC. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on.

Battery Care Portal is free, with all features included for now. It is for laptops with a battery.
```
