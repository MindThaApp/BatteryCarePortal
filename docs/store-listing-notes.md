# Store listing notes

## Crash reporting (for the listing text)

Battery Care Portal can send anonymous crash reports to help fix problems. This is off unless you turn it on, in the first-run welcome or in Settings. Reports are sent to Sentry and stored in the EU (Frankfurt). They contain no personal data, no battery serial numbers and no battery history, and no IP address is stored with them. Your battery history and settings stay on your PC.
